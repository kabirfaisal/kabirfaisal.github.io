Reading list

The references I point people to when they want the full version of the explainers above.

Books

  • Deep Learning — Goodfellow, Bengio & Courville. The standard reference; free at deeplearningbook.org.
  • Understanding Deep Learning — Simon Prince. Modern, visual; free PDF at udlbook.github.io.
  • The Elements of Statistical Learning — Hastie, Tibshirani & Friedman. Trees, boosting, the statistical view; free PDF.
  • Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning — Bishop. The probabilistic foundations.

Courses & sites

  • Dive into Deep Learning — interactive book with runnable code, d2l.ai.
  • fast.ai — practical deep learning, top-down.
  • Distill.pub — visual explanations of attention, momentum, and more.
  • The Illustrated Transformer — Jay Alammar’s walk-through of attention.

These notes are a personal reference and will grow over time. Corrections welcome — contact details are on the home page.
