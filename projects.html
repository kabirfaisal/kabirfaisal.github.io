KIT2

Decision support for pre-hospital emergency care

KIT2 built an AI-driven assistant for the German TeleNotArzt (telemedical emergency physician) service, where clinicians must make many critical decisions in very little time. The system takes a patient's heterogeneous record — time-series vital signs, tabular clinical fields, and unstructured free-text notes — and returns probabilistic rankings of likely diagnoses, suitable treatments, and the physiological measurements that should be taken next.

  • Deep multimodal learning architecture over time-series, tabular, and text inputs
  • Missing-value imputation for a real (non-public) emergency dataset — denoising LSTM autoencoders outperformed classical methods
  • Synthetic tabular data generation (WLSTM-GAN) to address privacy limits and data scarcity
  • Evaluated with recall@k, macro/weighted AUC, and coverage-ratio metrics
