<code class="formula">r  =  &Sigma;(X_i&minus;X&#772;)(Y_i&minus;Y&#772;)  /  ( &radic;&Sigma;(X_i&minus;X&#772;)&sup2; &middot; &radic;&Sigma;(Y_i&minus;Y&#772;)&sup2; )</code>
<p>Linear correlation between two rating vectors, e.g. two users&rsquo; ratings on the same items. Ranges &minus;1 to 1; the classic weight for user-based collaborative filtering.</p>
