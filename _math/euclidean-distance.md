title: "Euclidean distance"
---
<code class="formula">d(A,B)  =  &radic;( &Sigma; (A_i &minus; B_i)&sup2; )</code>
<p>Straight-line distance between two points in feature space; 0 means identical. Used to compare numeric-attribute profiles rather than orientation alone.</p>
