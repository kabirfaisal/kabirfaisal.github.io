title: "Precision@k, recall@k &amp; F1@k"
---
<code class="formula">Precision@k  =  (# relevant in top-k) / k
Recall@k     =  | Relevant &cap; Top-k | / | Relevant |
F1@k         =  2 &times; (Precision@k &times; Recall@k) / (Precision@k + Recall@k)</code>
<p>The survey&rsquo;s own notation for the two metrics I used in the TeleNotArzt paper, plus F1@k, their harmonic mean &mdash; a single number when you need one ranking headline instead of the precision/recall trade-off.</p>
