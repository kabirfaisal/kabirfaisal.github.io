title: "Gradient descent"
---
<code class="formula">&theta;  &larr;  &theta;  &minus;  &eta; &middot; &nabla;<sub>&theta;</sub> L(&theta;)</code>
<p>Repeatedly step the parameters <span class="mono">&theta;</span> a small amount (<span class="mono">&eta;</span>, the learning rate) in the direction that most decreases the loss. Backpropagation is just how <span class="mono">&nabla;<sub>&theta;</sub> L</span> is computed for a neural network.</p>
