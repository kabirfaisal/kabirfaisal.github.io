Diagnosis and Treatment Support as an AI-Driven Assistant System for TeleNotary Emergency Care

2026 M. F. Kabir, K. Piliuk, S. Tomforde

Proceedings of the 18th International Conference on Agents and Artificial Intelligence (ICAART), INSTICC / SciTePress. Vol. 5, pp. 4651–4660. ISBN 978-989-758-796-2.

A deep multimodal architecture (time-series signals, tabular clinical records, and free-text notes) that produces probabilistic rankings of likely diagnoses, therapeutic interventions, and required physiological measurements for pre-hospital TeleNotArzt care. Evaluated with recall@k, macro/weighted AUC, and coverage-ratio metrics.

Kabir, M. F., Piliuk, K., & Tomforde, S. (2026). Diagnosis and treatment support as AI-driven assistant system for TeleNotary emergency care. In Proceedings of the 18th International Conference on Agents and Artificial Intelligence — Volume 5: ICAART, INSTICC, SciTePress, pp. 4651–4660.

Synthetic Data Generation for Emergency Medical Systems: A Systematic Comparison of Tabular GAN Extensions

2025 M. F. Kabir, M. M. I. Nayem, S. Tomforde

Proceedings of the 17th International Conference on Agents and Artificial Intelligence (ICAART), Volume 3, INSTICC / SciTePress, pp. 1199–1206. ISBN 978-989-758-737-5.

Introduces WLSTM-GAN — a single generator with two specialised LSTM networks and separate discriminators for continuous and categorical features — and systematically compares tabular GAN variants on three medical datasets, outperforming baselines on fidelity and privacy preservation for emergency medical data.

Kabir, M. F., Nayem, M. M. I., & Tomforde, S. (2025). Synthetic data generation for emergency medical systems: A systematic comparison of tabular GAN extensions. In Proceedings of the 17th International Conference on Agents and Artificial Intelligence — Volume 3: ICAART, INSTICC, SciTePress, pp. 1199–1206.

A Deep Analysis for Medical Emergency Missing Value Imputation

2024 M. F. Kabir, S. Tomforde

Proceedings of the 16th International Conference on Agents and Artificial Intelligence (ICAART), Volume 3, INSTICC / SciTePress, pp. 1229–1236. ISBN 978-989-758-680-4.

Compares classical machine-learning imputation with denoising autoencoders and denoising LSTM autoencoders for numeric values missing completely at random in a real (non-public) telenotary emergency dataset with strong class imbalance and outliers. The denoising LSTM autoencoder performs best.

Kabir, M. F., & Tomforde, S. (2024). A deep analysis for medical emergency missing value imputation. In Proceedings of the 16th International Conference on Agents and Artificial Intelligence — Volume 3: ICAART, INSTICC, SciTePress, pp. 1229–1236.
