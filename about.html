Academic profile

About me

Research Assistant in the Intelligent Systems Group at Kiel University and PhD student, developing a recommendation system for diagnosis support in emergency medicine.

Who I am

I have hands-on experience in medical and clinical data analysis, data structuring, and prediction and classification modelling, with a strong technical background in Python, SQL, and diverse data formats, and practical experience in interdisciplinary academic and industrial environments.

The main focus of my PhD is developing a recommendation system for diagnosis support in emergency medicine — a system that helps clinicians reach a diagnosis from incomplete, heterogeneous, and time-critical patient data.

Since 2022 I have been a Research Assistant in the Intelligent Systems Group at Kiel University. I work on medical and clinical data for basic analysis, structuring, and data-flow management; curate research data from clinical and biomedical studies; build data-integration pipelines across text, image, tabular, and time-series sources; and develop predictive and classification models for diseases, medications, and measurements — ensuring data quality, consistency, and traceability across projects. I completed the KIT2 (TeleNotArzt) project, a real-world collaboration with RWTH Aachen University.

Before Kiel I was a Data Scientist in the Research & Development department of Paul Wurth S.A. in Luxembourg (2020–2021), working on data collection and validation, predictive modelling, and process optimisation with machine learning and deep learning for industrial projects.
