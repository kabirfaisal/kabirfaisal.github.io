About

Data science for time-critical healthcare

I have hands-on experience in medical and clinical data analysis, data structuring, and prediction and classification modelling, with a strong technical background in Python, SQL, and diverse data formats.

Since 2022 I have been a Research Assistant in the Intelligent Systems Group at Kiel University, working on clinical and biomedical research data — building data-integration pipelines across text, image, tabular, and time-series sources; ensuring data quality, consistency, and traceability; and developing predictive and classification models. As part of this work I completed the KIT2 (TeleNotArzt) project, a real-world collaboration with RWTH Aachen University. Previously I was a Data Scientist in the R&D department of Paul Wurth S.A. in Luxembourg. My PhD at Christian-Albrechts-Universität zu Kiel focuses on developing a recommendation system for diagnosis support in emergency medicine, built on AI-supported integration of multimodal clinical data.
