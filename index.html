My PhD research centres on developing a recommendation system for diagnosis support in emergency medicine. To get there I work on AI-supported integration of heterogeneous clinical data — multimodal deep learning across text, image, tabular, and time-series sources, together with synthetic data generation and missing-value imputation.

About

Data science for time-critical healthcare

My PhD at Christian-Albrechts-Universität zu Kiel is a recommendation system for diagnosis support in emergency medicine, built on AI-supported integration of multimodal clinical data. I work in the Intelligent Systems Group at Kiel University on machine learning, deep learning, and clinical data quality.
