<p>TabDDPM brings denoising diffusion &mdash; the family of models behind modern image generators &mdash; to mixed-type tables. Numerical columns get Gaussian diffusion, categorical columns get multinomial diffusion, and a single network learns to reverse the noising process. On many benchmarks it produces synthetic data whose downstream machine-learning utility beats GAN-based synthesizers.</p>
<p><strong>Why it is on my list:</strong> it is the strongest non-GAN alternative to WLSTM-GAN and a candidate for future comparison work.</p>
